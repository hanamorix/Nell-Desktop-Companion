# UserBubble

Your own message, shown on **cream paper** against Nell's plum bubbles (the user's choice in v3, replacing blue).

## Look
`user-fill` (`cream`) fill, `user-edge` (`snow`) top edge, a `muted` bottom bevel and `user-ink` (`outline`) text. Code chips keep their `plum-900` ground with `snow` text. Your bubbles are **right-aligned** in the column with the tail on the right, 12px in; every Nell bubble is left-aligned with its tail on the left.

## Rules
- It is added when you submit from the bar, or on `UserPromptSubmit` for text typed in kitty.
- It fades on the same clock as replies and follows the **BubbleStack** age ladder.

## What the consumer provides
The text.
